## LinkedIn

Screenshot → paste → describe. That is how most people ask an AI about an Instagram post today, and the context dies on the way.

We built Grammy in a 4-hour hackathon: a Chrome extension that puts a small mascot on Instagram and answers by voice about the post you are actually looking at. Two personas: a casual friend who explains what is on screen, and an influencer analyst who cites the likes, comments and hashtags it can see and ends with concrete recommendations. Say "remember this post's style" and it does, locally, with a delete button next to every memory.

What we were careful about: it reads only what Instagram already rendered, never clicks or posts for you, refuses private accounts, takes your own photos as input only, and generates no images. Nineteen failure modes map to one status pill.
